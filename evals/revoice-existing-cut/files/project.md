# Backup explainer

## Brief
> "make a short, plain explainer about why saved files go missing and how a backup fixes it"

## Audience and goal
People who keep their work on one laptop, on YouTube. One takeaway: files lost to sync can be protected in a minute.

## Look
- **Idea:** one file icon slips out of a folder, then sits safe under a small shield.
- **Palette:** slate #1E293B, mist #E2E8F0, paper #F8FAFC; ink #0F172A; amber #F59E0B.
- **Sound:** narration (v1 voice: bright and quick), a soft piano loop ducked under speech,
  whooshes on beat changes, a chime on "fixed.".
